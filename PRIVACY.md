# Privacy Policy

**Domino Score Tracker** (Arabic: سكور الدومينو)

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how the Domino Score Tracker Android app handles your information.

## Summary

The app does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What the app stores

To keep score, the app saves the following **only on your device**, in its private storage:

- Player names you type in
- Games, rounds, scores and game history
- Statistics built from your finished games
- Your settings, such as language, sounds and music

This data never leaves your device through the app. There is no account and no sign-in.

## What the app does not do

- It has no internet access. It does not request the `INTERNET` permission.
- It does not show ads.
- It does not use analytics, crash reporting or any third-party tracking SDK.
- It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
- It does not sell or share data with anyone.

## Sharing a result

When you tap **Share**, the app creates an image of the game result and opens the Android share sheet. You choose the app that receives it (for example a messaging app). After that, the image is handled by the app you picked and by its own privacy policy. Nothing is shared unless you start it.

## Device backup

Android can include app data in your device backup to your own Google account, if backup is turned on in your phone settings. This is controlled by you and by Android. The developer cannot access these backups.

## Deleting your data

To remove all app data, open **Settings > Apps > Domino Score Tracker > Storage > Clear data**, or uninstall the app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
